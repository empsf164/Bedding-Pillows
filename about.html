Our Brand Story

Better Sleep Starts With Better Comfort.

NESTORA was born out of a simple realization: your bed is the single most important piece of architecture in your daily life. We spend a third of our lives resting—it deserves pure natural fibers and heirloom craftsmanship.

Design Philosophy

Tactile Rest, Not Synthetic Shortcuts.

We reject synthetic polyester blends and chemical softening agents that wear down after a few washes. Instead, we collaborate directly with generational weavers in Guimarães, Portugal, and master textile artisans in northern Italy.

Every seam, every French envelope closure, and every stitch is calibrated for tactile longevity. Our bedding is designed to look effortlessly editorial when made, and cozy and lived-in when slept in.

Our Raw Materials

Ethically sourced, sustainably harvested, rigorously tested.

French Flax Linen

Cultivated in Normandy using zero irrigation. Naturally hollow fibers offer unmatched breathability across all four seasons.

Hungarian Goose Down

RDS certified 800 fill power down harvested humanely. Hypoallergenic washed in pristine alpine spring water.

Natural Talalay Latex

Tapped from sustainably managed Hevea Brasiliensis trees. Naturally buoyant, antimicrobial, and dust mite resistant.

GOTS Organic Cotton

Long-staple combed cotton grown without synthetic pesticides or genetic modification. Gentle on sensitive skin.

Our Planet Pledge

Conscious Luxury for Generations

We believe true luxury never comes at the cost of our planet. All our packaging is 100% plastic-free, made from biodegradable recycled card and reusable cotton bedding storage totes.

We offset 100% of carbon emissions generated from shipping through verified reforestation projects in the Pacific Northwest and southern Europe.
