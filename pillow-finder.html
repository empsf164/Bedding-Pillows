Sleep Science & Alignment

The Anatomy of Pillow Loft & Spinal Support

A pillow is not just a cushion—it is the orthopedic foundation that keeps your cervical spine aligned with your mattress all night.

Side Sleepers
High Loft (5"–6")

Fills the wide space between your shoulder point and ear, preventing lateral neck tilting and morning shoulder stiffness.

Back Sleepers
Medium Loft (3.5"–4.5")

Cradles the natural cervical curve of your neck without pitching your head forward, keeping airway passages naturally open.

Stomach Sleepers
Low Loft (2"–3")

Provides gentle, low-compression cushioning that prevents hyperextension of the upper back and neck throughout the night.

Hot Sleepers
Cooling Core

Pin-core ventilated natural latex and breathable organic cotton covers dissipate excess cranial heat for deep uninterrupted REM sleep.
